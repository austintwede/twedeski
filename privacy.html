Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

TwedeSki is a ski journal app made by Austin Twede. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. TwedeSki has no ads, does not sell your data, and does not use analytics or tracking services.

Your account

When you first open TwedeSki it creates an account for you without asking for anything. If you choose to save your account, we store your email address so you can sign in on a new phone. Your profile holds the name you enter, a friend code, and your resort wish list.

Location

  • While you record a ski day, TwedeSki uses your precise location, including in the background with the screen off, to track your runs, lifts, climbs, vertical and speed. Your GPS track is saved on your phone. The day's totals (runs, vertical, top speed, distance), its date and resort, and for backcountry days the general place you started, are saved to your account and visible to your friends.
  • Live sharing is off unless you turn it on. For each day you choose who can see where you are: only you (the default), your friends, or everyone at that resort (without your name). When you share, your current position and today's track are sent to our server and removed when you stop recording.
  • Arrival reminders, if you turn them on, use your location in the background to notice when you reach a ski resort and remind you to record. This happens on your phone; your location is not sent anywhere for reminders.
  • When you start recording or sort resorts by distance, your location is used on your phone to find the nearest resort.

Contacts

If you tap Find friends and allow access, TwedeSki reads your contacts on your phone. Email addresses are converted to one-way hashes on your phone and only the hashes are sent, to find which contacts already use TwedeSki. We don't store the hashes, names or numbers. Invites you send go through your own messaging app.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan a friend's QR code. Images never leave your phone.

What you write

Resorts you've skied, ski days, journal notes, reviews, deals, prices you paid and your wish list are saved to your account so they're backed up and your friends can see them. Only people you've added as friends can see them.

Feedback you send from the 💬 button goes to the developer with your name, the screen you sent it from and the app version. It's read with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to make the changes you ask for. Don't put private details in feedback.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts your account and data.
  • Expo delivers app updates.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather and snow; we send it a resort's (or a backcountry spot's) coordinates and date, not who you are.
  • OpenTopoMap and OpenSnowMap provide map images; your phone requests the map area you're looking at.

These services receive your IP address when your phone connects to them, as any website would.

Notifications

TwedeSki can notify you when a friend adds you, when a friend logs a ski day or starts skiing, and when you're at a resort. These are created on your phone.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it from our server and from your phone. If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion. You can also export everything you've saved from Settings at any time.

Children

TwedeSki is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact
