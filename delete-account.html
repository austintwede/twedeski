Delete your TwedeSki account

In the app

  1. Open TwedeSki and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email atweedybird@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my TwedeSki account”, from the email address you saved your account with (or include your friend code, shown under My code in the app). We'll delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

Everything: your profile and name, email, friend code, friendships and friend requests, resorts, ski days, journal notes, reviews, deals, wish list and any live location. Nothing is kept. Deleting in the app also erases the copy on your phone, including your GPS tracks.

Want a copy first? In Settings, tap Export to save everything to a file.
